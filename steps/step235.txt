What is a Cron Job?

A cron job is a scheduled task that automatically runs at specified times or intervals. It’s like setting an alarm to do a task repeatedly without you having to trigger it manually.

In our example, we will be sending a GET request every 14 minutes to the URL using the cron job there.

Examples Explained -

(1) */14 * * * * – Every 14 minutes

*/14 → Every 14 minutes
* (hour) → every hour
* (day of month) → every day
* (month) → every month
* (day of week) → every day of the week

Result: runs at 0, 14, 28, 42, 56 minutes of every hour.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
(2) 0 0 * * 0 – At midnight on every Sunday

0 (minute) → at minute 0
0 (hour) → at 0 hour (midnight)
* * → every day of month & every month
0 (day of week) → Sunday

Result: runs once every week at Sunday 00:00.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
(3) 30 3 15 * * – At 3:30 AM, on the 15th of every month

30 (minute) → at 30 minutes
3 (hour) → at 3 AM
15 (day of month) → on 15th
* * → every month, every day of week

Result: runs once a month at 03:30 on the 15th.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
(4) 0 0 1 1 * – At midnight, on January 1st

0 (minute) → at 0 minutes
0 (hour) → at 0 hour
1 (day of month) → 1st
1 (month) → January

* (day of week) → any day

Result: runs once a year on January 1st at 00:00.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
(5) 0 * * * * – Every hour

0 (minute) → at minute 0
* (hour) → every hour

Result: runs at the start of every hour.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------

cron.CronJob("*/14 * * * *", function () { ... }) → schedules the task every 14 minutes.

Inside the function, it sends a GET request to the URL in your environment variable.

And : Logs success or failure to the console.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------

So every 14 minutes, this job automatically runs without manual intervention.
